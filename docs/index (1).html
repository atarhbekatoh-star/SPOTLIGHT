The Process

How Spotlight
Works

01

Create Your Profile

Tell Spotlight your current confidence level, goals, and the situations you find most challenging — public speaking, networking, interviews, or everyday conversations.

02

Receive Daily Missions

Every day you get a new mission tailored to your skill level. From starting a conversation with a stranger to delivering a 2-minute speech — each task is a real-world challenge.

03

Complete & Reflect

Mark your mission done and log how it went. Spotlight tracks your emotional response, your growth, and adjusts future missions to push you just the right amount.

04

Level Up Your Confidence

Earn XP, unlock harder challenges, and watch your confidence score rise week over week. You're not just using an app — you're becoming someone new.
